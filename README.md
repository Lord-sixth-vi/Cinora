## What's in Cinora 1.0 Aurora

**Watching**
- YouTube home feed, search and channel pages
- Full video player: gestures, quality choice, subtitles, picture-in-picture, background play and resume where you left off
- Skips sponsor segments (SponsorBlock), privately

**Built to feel fast**
- Netflix-style look with smooth 120Hz motion
- Lite mode for older or slower phones

**Private by design**
- No analytics, no ads, no Google Play Services
- Crash reports stay on your phone and are only sent if you choose to
- Optional backup servers are off unless you switch them on

**Always up to date**
- Cinora checks for new versions by itself and installs them in a tap

More arrives one group per update: Library, Shorts, Downloads, Ambience, Music modes, Add-ons and Together mode.

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open the [latest release](https://github.com/Lord-sixth-vi/Cinora/releases/latest) and tap the `.apk` file.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
3. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer.

## Updates

Cinora checks this page for new versions when it opens (you can turn this off in **Settings → About**). When an update is out you'll see it with its notes; tap **Update** and Android installs it over the old version. Your settings stay.

## FAQ

**Is it on the Play Store?** No. Cinora is only released here.

**Why does Android warn me when installing?** Android shows that for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store.

**Does Cinora collect my data?** No. See **Settings → About → Privacy** in the app for exactly what each outside service (YouTube, SponsorBlock, GitHub for updates) can see.

**Something broke.** Open **Settings → About → Report a bug**, or [open an issue](https://github.com/Lord-sixth-vi/Cinora/issues/new).
