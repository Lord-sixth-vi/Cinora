## 🍿 Why Cinora?

> Most video apps watch you more than you watch them. 👀
> Cinora just plays your videos, fast, and then minds its own business.

- 🕵️ **No tracking, no telemetry, no ads.** We don't know what you watch. We don't *want* to know.
- ⚡ **Fast.** Tap a video and it starts. No popcorn-making time required.
- 🧈 **Buttery smooth.** 120Hz motion so silky it should come with a warning label.
- 🪶 **Tiny.** A few MB. Your storage will barely notice it moved in.
- 🧠 **Recommendations made on your phone,** not on someone's server farm.

## ✨ What's inside

### 🎥 Watching
- 🏠 YouTube home feed, search and channel pages
- 🔎 A search bar on every channel tab: find *that* video from 2016 in seconds
- 👎 Dislike counts are back (yes, really)
- 🏷️ Honest titles and thumbnails instead of clickbait (no more "YOU WON'T BELIEVE…")
- ⏭️ Skips sponsor segments automatically, privately
- 🎵 Lyrics on music videos, for your shower concerts

### 🕹️ The player
- 👆 Press and hold for **2x**, then slide up or down for **1.5x · 2x · 2.5x · 3x**. Lectures, meet your match.
- ☀️ Smooth brightness and 🔊 volume swipes (brightness only changes Cinora, never your whole phone)
- ⏪ Double-tap to skip, and keep tapping: +10, +20, +30…
- 🖼️ Picture-in-picture, 🎧 background play, and it remembers where you stopped
- 🌈 Black bars in full screen glow with the video instead of just… being black
- 🎚️ Fancy seek bars: wavy, neon, galaxy, lava, vinyl… plus little animated buddies that walk along as you watch
- 😴 Sleep timer, for when "one more video" becomes a lie
- 📺 Cast to your TV, no Google needed
- 🎛️ Equalizer, for bass you can feel in your soul

### 🛡️ Private by design
- 🚫 No analytics, no ads, no Google Play Services
- 🗂️ Crash reports stay on your phone; they're only sent if *you* tap Send
- 🔌 Backup servers stay off unless you switch them on

### 🔋 Kind to every phone
- 🐢➡️🐇 **Lite mode** turns off the eye candy so older phones run like new ones
- 🔄 Updates itself: one tap, done, your settings stay put

## 📲 Install in 30 seconds

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Lord-sixth-vi/Cinora/releases/latest) on your Android phone and tap the `.apk` file.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager. *(It's being protective. We like that.)*
3. Open the file, tap **Install**, and you're in. 🎉

Needs **Android 7.0 or newer**.

## 🔄 Updates

Cinora checks for new versions when it opens. When one's out, you'll see what's new. Tap **Update** and it installs right over the old one; your settings stay exactly where you left them. Don't want automatic checks? Turn them off in **Settings → About**.

## 🤔 FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it on the Play Store?</b></summary>

Nope. Cinora lives right here on GitHub. Think of it as a hidden café that only locals know about. ☕
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does Android warn me when installing?</b></summary>

Android shows that warning for every app that isn't from the Play Store. It's not personal. 😅
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Cinora collect my data?</b></summary>

No. Not a little. Not "anonymised". Not "just to improve your experience". **No.**
For exactly what each outside service can see (YouTube, SponsorBlock, GitHub for updates), open **Settings → About → Privacy** in the app.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>My phone is old. Will it work?</b></summary>

If it runs Android 7.0 or newer, yes. Turn on **Lite mode** and it'll feel younger too. 👴➡️🧒
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Something broke! 😱</b></summary>

Sorry about that! Open **Settings → About → Report a bug**, or [open an issue](https://github.com/Lord-sixth-vi/Cinora/issues/new). If Cinora crashed, it'll offer to attach the crash log, and only if you say yes.
</details>
