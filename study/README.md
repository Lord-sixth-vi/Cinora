# Study mode lists

Cinora's Study mode reads these two files, so new exams and free course sources can be added without an app update. The app checks for a newer copy about once a week and keeps its built-in copy if this one can't be read.

- `exams.json`: every exam in the Competitive exams tab, with its category chips (Group A–D, Central govt, State govt, PSU, Private jobs, Entrance, Professional, International) and its subjects and topics.
- `free_courses.json`: the free course sources in the Free courses tab (NPTEL, SWAYAM, Khan Academy, MIT OpenCourseWare, freeCodeCamp and others), matched by channel name, with the subjects and levels to browse by.

Spotted a missing exam or source? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Lord-sixth-vi/Cinora/issues/new).
